## 2005 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜心理与行为（猴子的公平感）
> Everybody loves a fat pay rise. Yet pleasure at your own can vanish if you learn that a colleague has been given a bigger one. Indeed, if he has a reputation for slacking, you might even be outraged. Such behaviour is regarded as "all too human," with the underlying assumption that other animals would not be capable of this finely developed sense of grievance. But a study by Sarah Brosnan and Frans de Waal of Emory University in Atlanta, Georgia, which has just been published in Nature, suggests that it is all too monkey, as well.
>
> Above all, like their female human counterparts, they tend to pay much closer attention to the value of "goods and services" than males. ... Indeed, the mere presence of a grape in the other chamber (without an actual monkey to eat it) was enough to induce resentment in a female capuchin. ... Feelings of righteous indignation, it seems, are not the preserve of people alone. Refusing a lesser reward completely makes these feelings abundantly clear to other members of the group. However, whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.

**可回溯语言点**：all too human（人之常情，含无奈语气）[a001]、finely developed sense of grievance（高度发达的不平感）、reputation for slacking（懒散之名）、outraged（愤慨）、counterparts（对应者）、luxury goods（奢侈品）、induce resentment（激起怨恨）、righteous indignation（义愤）[a028]、preserve＝专属领域[ a041 ]、stems from（源自）、as yet（迄今）、长难句 whether ... or whether ... is an unanswered question。

### 阅读 Text 2｜科学与技术（吸烟与怀疑论 / 全球变暖）
> Do you remember all those years when scientists argued that smoking would kill us but the doubters insisted that we didn't know for sure? That the evidence was inconclusive, the science uncertain? That the antismoking lobby was out to destroy our way of life and the government should stay out of the way? Lots of Americans bought that nonsense, and over three decades, some 10 million smokers went to early graves.
>
> There are upsetting parallels today, as scientists in one wave after another try to awaken us to the growing threat of global warming. ... Just as on smoking, voices now come from many quarters insisting that the science about global warming is incomplete. ... With the risks obvious and growing, a prudent people would take out an insurance policy now. ... Instead of a plan of action, they continue to press for more research -- a classic case of "paralysis by analysis." To serve as responsible stewards of the planet, we must press forward on deeper atmospheric and oceanic research.

**可回溯语言点**：inconclusive（无定论的）、be out to destroy（存心毁掉）[a002]、stay out of the way（不插手）[a003]、bought that nonsense＝轻信[ a051 ]、went to early graves（早亡）、upsetting parallels（令人不安的相似之处）、one wave after another（一波接一波）、a prudent people（审慎的民族）[a031]、take out an insurance policy（未雨绸缪）、paralysis by analysis（分析致瘫）[a004]、a classic case of（典型案例）、stewards of the planet（地球管家）、press for / press forward on（力促）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜医学与健康（梦与情绪）
> Of all the components of a good night's sleep, dreams seem to be least within our control. In dreams, a window opens into a world where logic is suspended and dead people speak. A century ago, Freud formulated his revolutionary theory that dreams were the disguised shadows of our unconscious desires and fears; by the late 1970s, neurologists had switched to thinking of them as just "mental noise" -- the random byproducts of the neural-repair work that goes on during sleep. Now researchers suspect that dreams are part of the mind's emotional thermostat, regulating moods while the brain is "off-line."
>
> But not all parts of the brain are equally involved; the limbic system (the "emotional brain") is especially active, while the prefrontal cortex (the center of intellect and reasoning) is relatively quiet. ... Most people seem to have more bad dreams early in the night, progressing toward happier ones before awakening, suggesting that they are working through negative feelings generated during the day.

**可回溯语言点**：within our control（可控）、logic is suspended（逻辑失效）、disguised shadows（伪装的影子）、formulated（创立<理论>）、mental noise / random byproducts、emotional thermostat（情绪恒温器）、limbic system（边缘系统）[a068]、prefrontal cortex（前额叶皮层）、work through（化解<情绪>）、be susceptible to（见 2008 Text1 同源考点）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜历史与语言（正式英语的消退）
> Americans no longer expect public figures, whether in speech or in writing, to command the English language with skill and gift. Nor do they aspire to such command themselves. In his latest book, Doing Our Own Thing: The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care, John McWhorter, a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views, sees the triumph of 1960s counter-culture as responsible for the decline of formal English.
>
> Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality is language history and change, and he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom," for example, to be natural and no more regrettable than the loss of the case-endings of Old English. But the cult of the authentic and the personal, "doing our own thing," has spelt the death of formal speech, writing, poetry and music. While even the modestly educated sought an elevated tone when they put pen to paper before the 1960s, even the most well regarded writing since then has sought to capture spoken English on the page. ... In both oral and written English, talking is triumphing over speaking, spontaneity over craft.
>
> Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture, the trend that Mr. McWhorter documents is unmistakable. ... As a linguist, he acknowledges that all varieties of human language, including non-standard ones like Black English, can be powerfully expressive -- there exists no language or dialect in the world that cannot convey complex ideas. ... Mr. McWhorter acknowledges that formal language is not strictly necessary, and proposes no radical education reforms -- he is really grieving over the loss of something beautiful more than useful. We now take our English "on paper plates instead of china."

**可回溯语言点**：public figures（公众人物）、command＝娴熟掌握[ a047 ]、aspire to（渴求）、degradation（退化）、controversialist（好争论者）[a029]、counter-culture（反主流文化）、permissive（放任的）、no more ... than ...（与……一样不）[a006]、case-endings（格词尾）、the cult of（对……的狂热崇拜）、spell the death of（宣告消亡）[a005 / a048]、spelt＝招致、elevated tone（高雅笔调）、put pen to paper（动笔）、performative genre（表演性体裁）、spontaneity over craft（随性压倒匠艺）[a030]、an array of（一系列）[a007]、document＝记录佐证[ a050 ]、grieving over（为……扼腕）、on paper plates instead of china（用纸盘取代瓷器，喻「美」的失落）。

## 2006 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜社会与人口（美国同化 homogenizing）
> In spite of "endless talk of difference," American society is an amazing machine for homogenizing people. There is "the democratizing uniformity of dress and discourse, and the casualness and absence of deference" characteristic of popular culture. ### 阅读 Text 2｜传媒与文化（莎士比亚剧团与小镇）
> Stratford-on-Avon, as we all know, has only one industry -- William Shakespeare -- but there are two distinctly separate and increasingly hostile branches. There is the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), which presents superb productions of the plays at the Shakespeare Memorial Theatre on the Avon. And there are the townsfolk who largely live off the tourists who come, not to see the plays, but to look at Anne Hathaway's Cottage, Shakespeare's birthplace and the other sights.
>
> The worthy residents of Stratford doubt that the theatre adds a penny to their revenue. ... It's all deliciously ironic when you consider that Shakespeare, who earns their living, was himself an actor (with a beard) and did his share of noise-making. ... It is the playgoers, the RSC contends, who bring in much of the town's revenue because they spend the night pouring cash into the hotels and restaurants. ... Stratford cries poor traditionally. Nevertheless every hotel in town seems to be adding a new wing or cocktail lounge. ... Anyway, the townsfolk can't understand why the Royal Shakespeare Company needs a subsidy. ... ### 阅读 Text 3｜环境与能源（渔业过度捕捞）
> When prehistoric man arrived in new parts of the world, something strange happened to the large animals. They suddenly became extinct. Smaller species survived. The large, slow-growing animals were easy game, and were quickly hunted to extinction. Now something similar could be happening in the oceans.
>
> That the seas are being overfished has been known for years. ... Their methods do not attempt to estimate the actual biomass (the amount of living biological matter) of fish species in particular parts of the ocean, but rather changes in that biomass over time. According to their latest paper published in Nature, the biomass of large predators (animals that kill and eat other animals) in a new fishery is reduced on average by 80% within 15 years of the start of exploitation. ... Dr. Worm acknowledges that the figures are conservative. ... Today's vessels can find their prey using satellites and sonar. ... Dr. Myers and Dr. Worm argue that their work gives a correct baseline, which future management efforts must take into account. ... the "shifting baseline." ... That matters because theory suggests that the maximum sustainable yield that can be cropped from a fishery comes when the biomass of a target species is about 50% of its original levels.

**可回溯语言点**：easy game＝易得手的猎物（与 2010 翻译 game 同考点）[a042]、became extinct（灭绝）、overfished（过度捕捞）、biomass（生物量）[a057]、large predators（大型捕食者）、conservative figures（保守数字）、prey（猎物）、sonar（声呐）、satellites、an underestimate of fish stocks（低估鱼类存量）、shifting baseline（移位基线）、take into account（纳入考量）[a014]、maximum sustainable yield（最大可持续产量）[a058]、crop from（从……捕取）、a bad way to do business。

### 阅读 Text 4｜传媒与文化（艺术与反快乐）
> Many things make people think artists are weird and the weirdest may be this: artists' only job is to explore emotions, and yet they choose to focus on the ones that feel bad. ... The earliest forms of art, like painting and music, are those best suited for expressing joy. But somewhere in the 19th century, more artists began seeing happiness as insipid, phony or, worst of all, boring as we went from Wordsworth's daffodils to Baudelaire's flowers of evil.
>
> You could argue that art became more skeptical of happiness because modern times have seen such misery. But it's not as if earlier times didn't know perpetual war, disaster and the massacre of innocents. The reason, in fact, may be just the opposite: there is too much damn happiness in the world today. ... The rise of anti-happy art almost exactly tracks the emergence of mass media, and with it, a commercial culture in which happiness is not just an ideal but an ideology. ... In the West, before mass communication and literacy, the most powerful mass medium was the church, which reminded worshippers that their souls were in peril and that they would someday be meat for worms. Given all this, they did not exactly need their art to be a bummer too. ... ## 2007 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜教育与人才（球星生日效应与刻意练习）
> If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006's World Cup tournament, you would most likely find a noteworthy quirk: elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months. If you then examined the European national youth teams that feed the World Cup and professional ranks, you would find this strange phenomenon to be even more pronounced.
>
> What might account for this strange phenomenon? Here are a few guesses: a) certain astrological signs confer superior soccer skills; b) winter-born babies tend to have higher oxygen capacity, which increases soccer stamina; c) soccer-mad parents are more likely to conceive in springtime, at the annual peak of soccer mania; d) none of the above. ... This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one. ... And the best way to learn how to encode information meaningfully, Ericsson determined, was a process known as deliberate practice.
>
> Their work makes a rather startling assertion: the trait we commonly call talent is highly overrated. Or, put another way, expert performers -- whether in memory or surgery, ballet or computer programming -- are nearly always made, not born.

**可回溯语言点**：noteworthy quirk（值得注意的怪现象）、elite（顶尖的）、pronounced（显著的）、account for（解释）、confer（赋予）、stamina（耐力）、soccer mania（足球狂热）、coupled with（加之）、cognitive vs intuitive（认知的 vs 直觉的）、are swamped by（被……盖过）、encode（编码）、deliberate practice（刻意练习）、entails more than（不止于）、startling assertion（惊人论断）[a032]、highly overrated（被严重高估）、made, not born（后天造就而非天生）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜教育与人才（智商测试）
> For the past several years, the Sunday newspaper supplement Parade has featured a column called "Ask Marilyn." People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant, who at age 10 had tested at a mental level of someone about 23 years old; that gave her an IQ of 228 -- the highest score ever recorded. >

### 阅读 Text 3｜经济与商业（中产家庭的经济风险）
> During the past generation, the American middle-class family that once could count on hard work and fair play to keep itself financially secure has been transformed by economic risk and new realities. ### 阅读 Text 4｜法律与治理（数据泄露）

## 2008 · 英语一

## 2011 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜传媒与文化

> The decision of the New York Philharmonic to hire Alan Gilbert as its next music director has been the talk of the classical-music world ever since the sudden announcement of his appointment in 2009. ### 阅读 Text 2｜职场与就业

### 阅读 Text 3｜经济与商业

> The rough guide to marketing success used to be that you got what you paid for. No longer. While traditional "paid" media—such as television commercials and print advertisements—still play a major role, companies today can exploit many alternative forms of media. Consumers passionate about a product may create "earned" media by willingly promoting it to friends, and a company may leverage "owned" media by sending e-mail alerts about products and sales to customers registered with its Web site. The way consumers now approach the process of making purchase decisions means that marketing's impact stems from a broad range of factors beyond conventional paid media.
>
> This trend, which we believe is still in its infancy, effectively began with retailers and travel providers such as airlines and hotels and will no doubt go further.
>
> The same dramatic technological changes … have also increased the risk that passionate consumers will voice their opinions in quicker, more visible, and much more damaging ways. Such hijacked media are the opposite of earned media: an asset or campaign becomes hostage to consumers … who make negative allegations about a brand or product. … Toyota Motor, for example, alleviated some of the damage from its recall crisis earlier this year with a relatively quick and well-orchestrated social-media response campaign.

**可回溯语言点**：stem from（源于）、a broad range of（一系列广泛的）、in its infancy（尚处萌芽）、earned / owned / sold / hijacked media（赢得型／自有型／售出型／被劫持型媒体）、leverage（善用、撬动）、voice（表达，动词僻义）、hostage（受制于）、allegations（指控）、hijack（劫持，2013 Text 1 复现）、alleviate（减轻）、recall（召回，僻义）、well-orchestrated（精心策划的）。

**可回溯语言点**：provocative（煽动性／发人深省的）、chatter（议论）、anything less than（有悖于）、dampen（使扫兴，僻义）、gratification（满足）、past-tense condition（过去式状态／追认的幸福）、procreation（生育）、be equivalent to（等同于）、celebrity mom（明星妈妈）、lean on（依靠）、glamorous（光鲜的）、subconscious（潜意识的）。

### 阅读 Text 1｜心理与行为

> Come on—Everybody's doing it. That whispered message, half invitation and half forcing, is what most of us think of when we hear the words peer pressure. >

### 阅读 Text 3｜科学与技术

> In the idealized version of how science is done, facts about the world are waiting to be observed and collected by objective researchers who use the scientific method to carry out their work. But in the everyday practice of science, discovery frequently follows an ambiguous and complicated route. … Opportunities for misinterpretation, error, and self-deception abound. Consequently, discovery claims should be thought of as protoscience. … But it takes collective scrutiny and acceptance to transform a discovery claim into a mature discovery. This is the credibility process, through which the individual researcher's me, here, now becomes the community's anyone, anywhere, anytime.
>
> Within the complex social structure of the scientific community, researchers make discoveries; editors and reviewers act as gatekeepers by controlling the publication process … As a discovery claim works its way through the community, the interaction and confrontation between shared and competing beliefs … transforms an individual's discovery claim into the community's credible discovery. … Second, novelty itself frequently provokes disbelief. … In the end, credibility "happens" to a discovery claim—a process that corresponds to what philosopher Annette Baier has described as the commons of the mind.

**可回溯语言点**：ambiguous（模糊的）、abound（大量存在）、self-deception（自欺）、protoscience（前科学）、collective scrutiny（集体审查）、credibility process（可信化过程）、gatekeepers（把关人）、discovery claim（发现声明）、work one's way through（逐步通过）、novelty（新颖性）、commons of the mind（心智公地）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜教育与人才

**可回溯语言点**：in their prime（全盛期）、thriving（兴盛）、in the way of（就…而言）、left-of-centre（中间偏左）、patrol（把守，僻义）、backload（后置／变相延后支付，僻义）、egregiously（恶劣地）、drawn-out（旷日持久的）、clamp down（严厉压制）、stay put（安于原位）、high achievers（高成就者）、fat pay packets（丰厚薪酬）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜法律与治理

### 阅读 Text 1｜职场与就业

> In order to "change lives for the better" and reduce "dependency," George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer, introduced the "upfront work search" scheme. Only if the jobless arrive at the jobcentre with a CV, register for online job search, and start looking for work will they be eligible for benefit—and then they should report weekly rather than fortnightly. … There will now be a seven-day wait for the jobseeker's allowance. "Those first few days should be spent looking for work, not looking to sign on." … an obviously indulgent system that demands too little effort from the newly unemployed to find work, and subsidises laziness. What motivated him … was his zeal for "fundamental fairness"—protecting the taxpayer … ensuring that only the most deserving claimants received their benefits.
>
> Even the very phrase "jobseeker's allowance"—invented in 1996—is about redefining the unemployed as a "jobseeker" who had no mandatory right to a benefit … Instead, the claimant receives a time-limited "allowance," conditional on actively seeking a job; no entitlement and no insurance.

**可回溯语言点**：upfront work search（前置求职）、eligible for（有资格获得）、Only if 倒装、fortnightly（每两周一次）、sign on（登记领救济，僻义）、indulgent（纵容的）、subsidise（助长）、zeal（热忱）、claimant（申领人）、mandatory（强制的）、conditional on（以…为条件）、entitlement（应得权益）、insure oneself against（投保以防）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜法律与治理

> All around the world, lawyers generate more hostility than the members of any other profession—with the possible exception of journalism. But there are few places where clients have more grounds for complaint than America. During the decade before the economic crisis, spending on legal services in America grew twice as fast as inflation. The best lawyers made skyscrapers-full of money, tempting ever more students to pile into law schools. … Many of them instead become the kind of nuisance-lawsuit filer that makes the tort system a costly nightmare.
>
> Sensible ideas have been around for a long time, but the state-level bodies that govern the profession have been too conservative to implement them. One idea is to allow people to study law as an undergraduate degree. Another is to let students sit for the bar after only two years of law school. If the bar exam is truly a stern enough test for a would-be lawyer, those who can sit it earlier should be allowed to do so. … The other reason why costs are so high is the restrictive guild-like ownership structure of the business. … isolating lawyers from the pressure to make money rather than serve clients ethically.

**可回溯语言点**：hostility（敌意）、with the possible exception of（除…外）、grounds for complaint（抱怨的理由）、pile into（蜂拥进入）、nuisance-lawsuit filer（恶意诉讼提起人）、tort system（侵权法体系）、sit for the bar（参加律师资格考试，bar 僻义）、stern（严格的）、would-be（准／未来的）、cut their debt mountain by a third（减债三分之一）、guild-like（行会式的）、restrictive（限制性的）、isolate ... from（使隔离于）、liberalizing（自由化）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜科学与技术

> The US$3-million Fundamental Physics Prize is indeed an interesting experiment, as Alexander Polyakov said when he accepted this year's award in March. And it is far from the only one of its type. As a News Feature article in Nature discusses, a string of lucrative awards for researchers have joined the Nobel Prizes in recent years. … These benefactors have succeeded in their chosen fields, they say, and they want to use their wealth to draw attention to those who have succeeded in science.
>
> You cannot buy class, as the old saying goes, and these upstart entrepreneurs cannot buy their prizes the prestige of the Nobels. The new awards are an exercise in self-promotion for those behind them, say scientists. They could distort the achievement-based system of peer-review-led research. They could cement the status quo of peer-reviewed research. They do not fund peer-reviewed research. They perpetuate the myth of the lone genius. … But the Nobel Foundation's limit of three recipients per prize … has long been outgrown by the collaborative nature of modern research—as will be demonstrated by the inevitable row over who is ignored … Time, rather than intention, has given them legitimacy. … It is wise to take such gifts with gratitude and grace.

**可回溯语言点**：far from（远非）、a string of（一系列）、lucrative（丰厚的）、telephone-number-sized（数额巨大的）、benefactor（捐助人）、draw attention to（使关注）、buy class（买来格调，class 僻义）、upstart（暴发户式的）、prestige（声望）、an exercise in（…的体现）、self-promotion（自我宣传）、distort（扭曲）、cement the status quo（固化现状）、perpetuate（使长存）、outgrow（使过时，僻义）、row（争议，僻义）、Time ... has given them legitimacy（时间赋予正当性）、with gratitude and grace（心怀感激、体面地）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜教育与人才

> "The Heart of the Matter," the just-released report by the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS), deserves praise for affirming the importance of the humanities and social sciences to the prosperity and security of liberal democracy in America. … In 2010, leading congressional Democrats and Republicans sent letters to the AAAS asking that it identify actions … to "maintain national excellence in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education."
>
> To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for increased investment in research, the crafting of coherent curricula that improve students' ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century, increased funding for teachers and the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.

**可回溯语言点**：deserve praise for（因…值得称赞）、affirm（肯定）、the humanities（人文学科）、liberal democracy（自由民主）、just-released（刚发布的）、scholarship（学术研究，僻义）、coherent curricula（连贯课程，coherent 与 2016 Text 2 coherence 同源）、bring their learning to bear on（把学识运用于）、calls for（呼吁）。

## 2015 · 英语一

> 核实来源：新东方在线（Text 1）`https://kaoyan.koolearn.com/20141227/850751_2.html`；启航整卷真题 PDF（Text 2／3）`http://aged-jixun-cdn.iqihang.com/2024/1114/20241114023013669.pdf`；中国教育在线（Text 4）`https://kaoyan.eol.cn/shiti/yingyu/201605/t20160512_1397237_4.shtml`；新东方在线（Text 4）`https://kaoyan.koolearn.com/20141227/850753_4.html`；中公考研（Text 4）`https://wap.kaoyan365.cn/yingyu/zhenti/23581.html`；新航道（Text 1 逐句）`https://cs.xhd.cn/info/kyztk/957143.html`

### 阅读 Text 1｜社会与人口

> King Juan Carlos of Spain once insisted "kings don't abdicate, they die in their sleep." But embarrassing scandals and the popularity of the republican left in the recent Euro-elections have forced him to eat his words and stand down. So, does the Spanish crisis suggest that monarchy is seeing its last days? Does that mean the writing is on the wall for all European royals, with their magnificent uniforms and majestic lifestyles?
>
> When public opinion is particularly polarised, as it was following the end of the Franco regime, monarchs can rise above "mere" politics and "embody" a spirit of national unity. It is this apparent transcendence of politics that explains monarchs' continuing popularity. … But unlike their absolutist counterparts in the Gulf and Asia, most royal families have survived because they allow voters to avoid the difficult search for a non-controversial but respected public figure. … Even so, these are wealthy families who party with the international 1%, and media intrusiveness makes it increasingly difficult to maintain the right image. … He has failed to understand that monarchies have largely survived because they provide a service—as non-controversial and non-political heads of state.

**可回溯语言点**：abdicate（退位）、eat his words（收回前言）、stand down（退位）、see its last days（走向末日）、the writing is on the wall（不祥之兆）、monarchy（君主制）、polarised（两极分化的）、rise above（超越）、embody（体现）、transcendence（超越性）、monarch-infested（君主密集的）、absolutist counterparts（专制君主，counterpart 与 2016 Text 4 复现）、downside（弊端）、indefensible（无法辩护的）、inherited wealth（继承财富）、bizarre（荒诞的）、party with（与…交际，party 动词僻义）、media intrusiveness（媒体侵扰）、well-heeled（富有的）、hierarchical（等级森严的）、heads of state（国家元首）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜法律与治理

> Just how much does the Constitution protect your digital data? The Supreme Court will now consider whether police can search the contents of a mobile phone without a warrant if the phone is on or around a person during an arrest. California has asked the justices to refrain from a sweeping ruling, particularly one that upsets the old assumption that authorities may search through the possessions of suspects at the time of their arrest. It is hard, the state argues, for judges to assess the implications of new and rapidly changing technologies. The court would be recklessly modest if it followed California's advice. Enough of the implications are discernable, even obvious, so that the justices can and should provide updated guidelines to police, lawyers and defendants.
>
> They should start by discarding California's lame argument that exploring the contents of a smartphone—a vast storehouse of digital information—is similar to, say, going through a suspect's purse. … Citizens still have a right to expect private documents to remain private and protected by the Constitution's prohibition on unreasonable searches. As so often is the case, stating that principle doesn't ease the challenge of line-drawing. … But the justices should not swallow California's argument whole. New, disruptive technology sometimes demands novel applications of the Constitution's protections. … they must sort out how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now.

**可回溯语言点**：digital data（数字数据）、warrant（搜查令）、refrain from（避免）、a sweeping ruling（一刀切裁决）、upset the old assumption（推翻旧假定）、implications（影响）、recklessly modest（过分谨慎）、discernable（可辨识的）、lame argument（站不住脚的论据）、a vast storehouse（海量存储）、correspondence（通信往来，僻义）、prohibition on（禁止）、as so often is the case（一如常情）、line-drawing（划界）、swallow ... whole（照单全收）、disruptive（颠覆性的）、novel applications（崭新适用，novel 形容词僻义）、sort out（厘清）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜科学与技术

> The journal Science is adding an extra round of statistical checks to its peer-review process, editor-in-chief Marcia McNutt announced today. The policy follows similar efforts from other journals, after widespread concern that basic mistakes in data analysis are contributing to the irreproducibility of many published research findings. "Readers must have confidence in the conclusions published in our journal," writes McNutt in an editorial. Working with the American Statistical Association, the journal has appointed seven experts to a statistics board of reviewing editors (SBoRE). Manuscript will be flagged up for additional scrutiny by the journal's internal editors …
>
> Asked whether any particular papers had impelled the change, McNutt said: "The creation of the 'statistics board' was motivated by concerns broadly with the application of statistics and data analysis in scientific research and is part of Science's overall drive to increase reproducibility in the research we publish." … a larger group of publishing places that may want to model their approach after Science.

**可回溯语言点**：peer-review process（同行评审流程，2014 Text 3 复现）、irreproducibility（不可复现性）、editorial（社论，僻义）、flag up（标出）、additional scrutiny（额外审查，scrutiny 与 2012 Text 3 复现）、impel（促成）、overall drive（整体努力，drive 名词僻义）、advisory role（咨询角色）、foresight（远见）、model ... after（效仿）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜传媒与文化

> Two years ago, Rupert Murdoch's daughter, Elisabeth, spoke of the "unsettling dearth of integrity across so many of our institutions". Integrity had collapsed, she argued, because of a collective acceptance that the only "sorting mechanism" in society should be profit and the market. … Driving her point home, she continued: "It's increasingly apparent that the absence of purpose, of a moral language within government, media or business could become one of the most dangerous goals for capitalism and freedom." … As the hacking trial concludes … the wider issue of dearth of integrity still stands. Journalists are known to have hacked the phones of up to 5,500 people. This is hacking on an industrial scale. … In many respects, the dearth of moral purpose frames not only the fact of such widespread phone hacking but the terms on which the trial took place.
>
> In today's world, it has become normal that well-paid executives should not be accountable for what happens in the organisations that they run. … The words that have mattered are efficiency, flexibility, shareholder value, business-friendly, wealth generation, sales, impact and, in newspapers, circulation. Words degraded to the margin have been justice, fairness, tolerance, proportionality and accountability. … It was to ruin lives in the quest for circulation and impact.

**可回溯语言点**：dearth of（…的匮乏）、integrity（诚信）、collective acceptance（普遍接受）、sorting mechanism（筛选机制）、drive one's point home（把观点讲透）、moral language（道德话语）、wounding（损害）、lose its way（迷失方向）、conspire to hack phones（合谋窃听）、predecessor（前任）、on an industrial scale（工业化规模）、still unfolds（仍在展开）、frame（框定，动词僻义）、revelations（披露）、accountable（负有责任的，2012 完形复现）、shareholder value（股东价值）、circulation（发行量，僻义，2016 Text 4 复现）、degraded to the margin（被边缘化）、proportionality（相称性）、in the quest for（为追求）、traceable（可追溯的）。

## 2016 · 英语一

> 核实来源：中国教育在线（Text 1）`https://kaoyan.eol.cn/shiti/yingyu/201605/t20160512_1397148_2.shtml`；中国教育在线（Text 2）`https://kaoyan.eol.cn/shiti/yingyu/201605/t20160512_1397148_3.shtml`；中国教育在线（Text 4）`https://kaoyan.eol.cn/shiti/yingyu/201605/t20160512_1397148_5.shtml`；新东方考研网（Text 2／3）`https://kaoyan.xdf.cn/201512/10395976_2.html`；考研帮（Text 4）`https://m.kaoyan.com/www/yingyu/zhenti/567fb17d5b521_2.html`；真题逐句 docx `https://m.kyzs.com/kyyy/ziliao/2016年考研英语一真题及答案.docx`

### 阅读 Text 1｜社会与人口

> France, which prides itself as the global innovator of fashion, has decided its fashion industry has lost an absolute right to define physical beauty for women. Its lawmakers gave preliminary approval last week to a law that would make it a crime to employ ultra-thin models on runways. The parliament also agreed to ban websites that "incite excessive thinness" by promoting extreme dieting. Such measures have a couple of uplifting motives. They suggest beauty should not be defined by looks that end up impinging on health. … The bans, if fully enforced, would suggest to women (and many men) that they should not let others be arbiters of their beauty.
>
> In Denmark, the United States, and a few other countries, it is trying to set voluntary standards for models and fashion images that rely more on peer pressure for enforcement. … The charter's main tool of enforcement is to deny access for designers and modeling agencies to Copenhagen Fashion Week … But in general it relies on a name-and-shame method of compliance. Relying on ethical persuasion rather than law to address the misuse of body ideals may be the best step. Even better would be to help elevate notions of beauty beyond the material standards of a particular industry.

**可回溯语言点**：pride itself as（以…自居）、preliminary approval（初步通过）、incite（煽动）、impinging on（侵害，2016 词义题考点）、uplifting（积极的）、go beyond（不止于）、the social tape-measure（社会标尺）、arbiters（裁定者）、intangible（无形的）、skin-deep（肤浅的）、inherent（固有的）、adornment（装饰）、peer pressure（同辈压力，2012 Text 1 复现）、sanctions（制裁）、name-and-shame（点名羞辱）、compliance（遵从）、address the misuse（应对误用，address 僻义与 2012 完形复现）、elevate（提升）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜环境与能源

> For the first time in history more people live in towns than in the country. In Britain this has had a curious result. While polls show Britons rate "the countryside" alongside the royal family, Shakespeare and the National Health Service (NHS) as what makes them proudest of their country, this has limited political support. … It was specifically to provide city dwellers with spaces for leisure where they could experience "a refreshing air." Hill's pressures later led to the creation of national parks and green belts. They don't make countryside any more, and every year concrete consumes more of it. It needs constant guardianship. At the next election none of the big parties seem likely to endorse this sentiment.
>
> The Conservatives' planning reform explicitly gives rural development priority over conservation … The concept of sustainable development has been defined as profitable. … The idea that "housing crisis" equals "concreted meadows" is pure lobby talk. … Under lobby pressure, George Osborne favours rural new-build against urban renovation and renewal. … Development should be planned, not let rip. After the Netherlands, Britain is Europe's most crowded country. Half a century of town and country planning has enabled it to retain an enviable rural coherence … Avoiding this rather than promoting it should unite the left and right of the political spectrum.

**可回溯语言点**：rate ... alongside（把…与…并列）、curious result（耐人寻味的结果）、city dwellers（城市居民）、green belts（绿化带）、concrete（混凝土，此处名词）、constant guardianship（持续的守护，constant 形容词本义）、endorse（认可）、sentiment（情感）、give ... priority over（优先于）、sustainable development（可持续发展，与 2013 Text 1 sustainability 同根）、side with（支持）、plead for（呼吁）、strike terror into（使惊恐）、lobby talk（游说说辞，lobby 僻义）、biased（有偏见的）、let rip（放任自流）、rural coherence（乡村整体性，coherent 与 2014 Text 4 同源）、political spectrum（政治光谱）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜经济与商业

> "There is one and only one social responsibility of business," wrote Milton Friedman, a Nobel prize-winning economist, "That is, to use its resources and engage in activities designed to increase its profits." But even if you accept Friedman's premise and regard corporate social responsibility (CSR) policies as a waste of shareholders' money, things may not be absolutely clear-cut. New research suggests that CSR may create monetary value for companies—at least when they are prosecuted for corruption.
>
> And third, through a more diffuse "halo effect," whereby its good deeds earn it greater consideration from consumers and others. … The study found that, among prosecuted firms, those with the most comprehensive CSR programmes tended to get more lenient penalties. Their analysis ruled out the possibility that it was firms' political influence … that accounted for the leniency … In all, the study concludes that whereas prosecutors should only evaluate a case based on its merits, they do seem to be influenced by a company's record in CSR. … Nor does it reveal how much companies are banking on the halo effect … when they decide their do-gooding policies. But at least they have demonstrated that when companies get into trouble with the law, evidence of good character can win them a less costly punishment.

**可回溯语言点**：engage in（从事）、premise（前提）、corporate social responsibility (CSR)（企业社会责任）、clear-cut（泾渭分明的）、monetary value（货币价值）、prosecuted for corruption（因腐败被起诉）、a "signal"（信号）、good causes（公益事业）、diffuse（弥散的）、halo effect（光环效应）、whereby（借以）、lenient（宽大的，2016 词义题考点）、rule out（排除）、account for（解释）、on its merits（依据是非曲直）、bank on（依赖）、do-gooding（行善的）、get into trouble with the law（触法）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜传媒与文化

> There will eventually come a day when The New York Times ceases to publish stories on newsprint. … Nostalgia for ink on paper and the rustle of pages aside, there's plenty of incentive to ditch print. The infrastructure required to make a physical newspaper—printing presses, delivery trucks—isn't just expensive; it's excessive at a time when online-only competitors don't have the same set of financial constraints. Readers are migrating away from print anyway. And though print ad sales still dwarf their online and mobile counterparts, revenue from print is still declining. Overhead may be high and circulation lower, but rushing to eliminate its print edition would be a mistake …
>
> Peretti gives the example of Netflix discontinuing its DVD-mailing service to focus on streaming. "It was seen as a blunder," he said. The move turned out to be foresighted. … "I would raise prices and make it into more of a legacy product." … In other words, if you're going to make a print product, make it for the people who are already obsessed with it. … "In those situations, it's better to be more aggressive than less aggressive."

**可回溯语言点**：newsprint（新闻纸）、nostalgia for（对…的怀旧）、rustle（沙沙声）、aside（撇开不谈，后置）、incentive（动因）、ditch print（弃纸转网，ditch 动词僻义）、infrastructure（基础设施）、financial constraints（财务约束）、migrate away from（转移离开，僻义）、dwarf（远超，动词僻义）、counterparts（对应业务，2015 Text 1 复现）、revenue（营收）、overhead（管理费用，僻义）、circulation（发行量，2015 Text 4 复现）、go about doing it（着手处理）、blunder（蠢事）、foresighted（有先见之明的）、legacy product（传承产品）、be obsessed with（痴迷于）、subscription（订阅）、aggressive（进取的）。

## 分卷说明（口径与取舍）

- **范围**：英语一 2017、2018、2019、2020、2021、2022、2023、2024 共八套，以阅读 Part A 为主。任务允许「2025 若公开真题不足则收紧到 2017–2024」；2025 真题公开文本多为回忆版、版本差异大，按 SCHEMA 铁律 1/2（quote 必须为核实过的真题原句）**未收录任何 2025 句子**，分卷收紧到 2017–2024。
- **quote 来源**：全部句子经 WebSearch/WebFetch 多来源交叉核对；下方「核实来源链接」逐条列出。凡只找到一个来源、或不同来源措辞打架的句子，一律不写入 JSON。
- **题源版 ≠ 真题版**：2017 Text 2 的「题源解析」（中国教育在线 `t20161225_1478907`）是外刊原文（含 Kilo hōkū、Perched、dismantle、sanctity 等词），与考研删改后的真题文本（如 Rested / disassemble / holiness）不同。本分卷一律以**真题删改版**（eol `t20161224_1478832_*`、考研帮、cupl.cc 等）为准，题源版仅用于辨析，不作 quote。
- **回忆版处理**：2024 英语一 Text 1（罗马钉子 / Nuts and Bolts）公开页面均标注「回忆版」，且存在 `learnt/learned`、人名 `Sidel/Sichel`、`weapons-grade/weapon-grade`、句子增减等差异。本分卷**只收一条多来源完全一致的开头稳定片段**（`pull out of`），其余拿不准的句子不写。注意 2024 英语**二** Text 1（Diane Coyle《Cogs and Monsters》）与英语一不同，且部分站点把英语二文本误标为英语一，已剔除，未混入本分卷。
- **提示与真题不符处**：任务提示的「2021 Text3 文艺复兴与口红」经核实，2021 英语一 Text 3 实为**维多利亚人照片中的微笑**（Victorians smiling / prudery / dentistry），据官方 PDF 与多站一致原文收录；提示中「2020 Text1 英国文化之城」「2021 Text2 印尼森林与扶贫」「2019 Text4 消费税与网购」等未核实到可靠正式原文，**宁缺勿滥，未收录**。
- **熟词僻义取舍**：任务点名的 `pool / employ / ground / game / cure / constant / doctor / divide` 及 `climate` 的僻义，在 2017–2024 已核实真题文本中**未出现**（`groundless` 仅见于题目选项解析，`climate` 在 2023 Text 1 为「气候」常用义），按「只收这一段真题里真实出现且核实过的」不收；实际收录 `code / voluntary code / dismiss / embrace / buy / address / drag` 等确证僻义，另补 `giant / sound / preserve / becoming / take / pen / save for / provisions / secure / weep`。
- **考频**：`freq` = 本分卷内可回溯的独立真题出处数，`years_seen` 与之等长、只列真实年份；跨年份复现的出处在对应 `note` 中点明。

## 2017 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜机场安检长队（社会与人口）
> But demanding too much of air travelers or providing too little security in return undermines public support for the process. And it should: Wasted time is a drag on Americans' economic and private lives, not to mention infuriating.

> Part of the issue is that the government did not anticipate the steep increase in airline travel, so the TSA is now rushing to get new screeners on the line. Another factor may be that more people are trying to overpack their carry-on bags to avoid checked-baggage fees, though the airlines strongly dispute this.

**可回溯语言点**：`a drag on`（累赘/拖累，非「拖曳」）、`in return`（作为回报）、`undermine public support`（侵蚀公众支持）、`steep increase`（剧增）、`dispute`（质疑/反驳，非「争论」）、`be eligible to use expedited screening lanes`（有资格使用快速通道）、`sticker shock`（标价震惊）、`diverting resources into`（把资源挪用于）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜夏威夷望远镜与土著文化（传媒与文化）
> “The ancient Hawaiians were astronomers,” wrote Queen Liliuokalani, Hawaii's last reigning monarch, in 1897. Star watchers were among the most esteemed members of Hawaiian society. Sadly, all is not well with astronomy in Hawaii today. Protests have erupted over construction of the Thirty Meter Telescope (TMT), a giant observatory that promises to revolutionize humanity's view of the cosmos.

> At issue is the TMT's planned location on Mauna Kea, a dormant volcano worshiped by some Hawaiians as the piko, that connects the Hawaiian Islands to the heavens.

> Hawaiian culture is not a relic of the past; it is a living culture undergoing a renaissance today. … Perhaps that is why we explore the starry skies, as if answering a primal calling to know ourselves and our true ancestral homes.

> There is no reason why everyone cannot be welcomed on Mauna Kea to embrace their cultural heritage and to study the stars.

**可回溯语言点**：`esteemed`（受尊敬的）、`at issue`（争议焦点）、`dormant volcano`（休眠火山）、`unsurpassed clarity`（无与伦比的清晰度）、`renaissance`（复兴，非专指文艺复兴）、`a primal calling`（本原的召唤）、`embrace their cultural heritage`（拥抱文化遗产，非肢体动作）、`cultural heritage`、`fragile ecosystems`（脆弱生态）、`prioritize the protection`（优先保护）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜GDP 与幸福感（经济与商业）
> Robert F. Kennedy once said that a country's GDP measures “everything except that which makes life worthwhile.” … Many argue that it is a flawed concept. It measures things that do not matter and misses things that do. By most recent measures, the UK's GDP has been the envy of the Western world, with record low unemployment and high growth figures.

> A recent annual study of countries and their ability to convert growth into well-being sheds some light on that question. Across the 163 countries measured, the UK is one of the poorest performers in ensuring that economic growth is translated into meaningful improvements for its citizens.

> Yes, there has been a budding economic recovery since the 2008 global crash, but in key indicators in areas such as health and education, major economies have continued to decline. … When GDP is no longer regarded as the sole measure of a country's success, the world looks very different.

**可回溯语言点**：`shed light on`（阐明）、`convert growth into well-being`（把增长转化为福祉）、`be translated into`（被转化为，非「翻译」）、`a flawed concept`（有缺陷的概念）、`the envy of`（令…艳羡）、`a budding economic recovery`（初现的经济复苏）、`a more rounded assessment`（更全面的评估）、`the sole measure`（唯一标尺）、`well-being`（福祉，主题词）、`the forecasted doom`（预期的厄运）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜官员腐败与贿赂认定（法律与治理）
> In a rare unanimous ruling, the US Supreme Court has overturned the corruption conviction of a former Virginia governor, Robert McDonnell. But it did so while holding its nose at the ethics of his conduct, which included accepting gifts such as a Rolex watch and a Ferrari automobile from a company seeking access to government.

> The court did suggest that accepting favors in return for opening doors is “distasteful” and “nasty.” But under anti-bribery laws, proof must be made of concrete benefits, such as approval of a contract or regulation.

> Favoritism in official access can fan public perceptions of corruption. But it is not always corruption. … If connections can be bought, a basic premise of democratic society—that all are equal in treatment by government—is undermined. Good governance rests on an understanding of the inherent worth of each individual.

**可回溯语言点**：`in return for`（作为…的回报）、`a unanimous ruling`（一致裁决）、`overturn the conviction`（推翻定罪）、`holding its nose at`（对…嗤之以鼻）、`official acts`（公务行为）、`anti-bribery laws`（反贿赂法）、`concrete benefits`（具体利益）、`fan public perceptions`（助长公众观感，fan 作动词）、`play favorites`（厚此薄彼）、`government transparency`（政府透明度）、`If connections can be bought`（buy = 收买/买通）、`a basic premise`（基本前提）、`inherent worth`（固有价值）。

## 2018 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜机器人与中产就业（职场与就业）
> Among the annoying challenges facing the middle class is one that will probably go unmentioned in the next presidential campaign: What happens when the robots come for their jobs? Don't dismiss that possibility entirely. About half of U.S. jobs are at high risk of being automated, according to a University of Oxford study, with the middle class disproportionately squeezed.

> This isn't to be alarmist. Optimists point out that technological upheaval has benefited workers in the past. … Likewise, automation should eventually boost productivity, stimulate demand by driving down prices, and free workers from hard, boring work. But in the medium term, middle-class workers may need a lot of help adjusting.

> The challenge of coping with automation underlines the need for the U.S. to revive its fading business dynamism: Starting new companies must be made easier. … Destroying the machines that are coming for our jobs would be nuts. But policies to help workers adapt will be indispensable.

**可回溯语言点**：`dismiss`（排除/不予考虑，非「解雇」）、`disproportionately squeezed`（不成比例地受挤压）、`appeal to robots`（对…有吸引力）、`technological upheaval`（技术剧变）、`boost productivity`（提高生产率）、`in the medium term`（中期来看）、`underline the need for`（凸显…的必要）、`business dynamism`（商业活力）、`smooth the transition`（平稳过渡）、`the safety net`（社会保障网）、`upended by automation`（被自动化颠覆）、`indispensable`（不可或缺）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜社交媒体与假新闻（传媒与文化）
> A new survey by Harvard University finds more than two-thirds of young Americans disapprove of President Trump's use of Twitter. … Yet as distrust has risen toward all media, people may be starting to beef up their media literacy skills. Such a trend is badly needed.

> Young people who are digital natives are indeed becoming more skillful at separating fact from fiction in cyberspace. … They cross-check sources and prefer news from different perspectives—especially those that are open about any bias.

> “Many young people assume a great deal of personal responsibility for educating themselves and actively seeking out opposing viewpoints,” the survey concluded. … About a third say the problem of fake news lies in “misinterpretation or exaggeration of actual news” via social media.

> So when young people are critical of an over-tweeting president, they reveal a mental discipline in thinking skills—and in their choices on when to share on social media.

**可回溯语言点**：`beef up`（增强）、`media literacy`（媒介素养，主题词）、`badly needed`（亟需，badly = 非常）、`separating fact from fiction`（辨别真伪）、`distributed trust`（分布式信任）、`cross-check sources`（交叉核对来源）、`open about any bias`（对偏见公开）、`assume personal responsibility`（承担个人责任）、`seek out opposing viewpoints`（主动寻找对立观点）、`lie in`（在于）、`counteracting this problem`（应对/抵制）、`a mental discipline`（思维自律，discipline 非「纪律」）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜NHS 与 DeepMind 数据（科技与AI）
> Any fair-minded assessment of the dangers of the deal between Britain's National Health Service (NHS) and DeepMind must start by acknowledging that both sides mean well. … It is against that background that the information commissioner, Elizabeth Denham, has issued her damning verdict against the Royal Free hospital trust under the NHS, which handed over to DeepMind the records of 1.6 million patients in 2015 on the basis of a vague agreement which took far too little account of the patients' rights and their expectations of privacy.

> DeepMind has almost apologized. The NHS trust has mended its ways. Further arrangements—and there may be many—between the NHS and DeepMind will be carefully scrutinised to ensure that all necessary permissions have been asked of patients and all unnecessary data has been cleaned. There are lessons about informed patient consent to learn. But privacy is not the only angle in this case and not even the most important.

> But this distinction misses the point that it is processing and aggregation, not the mere possession of bits, that gives the data value. … That misses the way the surveillance economy works. The data of an individual there gains its value only when it is compared with the data of countless millions more.

> The use of privacy law to curb the tech giants in this instance feels slightly maladapted. This practice does not address the real worry. … What matters is that they will belong to a private monopoly which developed them using public resources. … A long struggle will be needed to avoid a future of digital feudalism.

**可回溯语言点**：`fair-minded`（公正的）、`a damning verdict`（严厉谴责的裁决）、`take too little account of`（太少考虑）、`mend its ways`（改过）、`scrutinise`（细查）、`informed patient consent`（知情同意）、`angle`（方面，非「角度」）、`miss the point`（没抓住要点）、`aggregation`（聚合）、`surveillance economy`（监控经济）、`curb`（遏制）、`maladapted`（错配的）、`address the real worry`（处理真正的担忧）、`a private monopoly`（私人垄断）、`digital feudalism`（数字封建主义）、`algorithm`（算法，主题词）。

### 阅读 Text 4｜美国邮政改革（经济与商业）
> The U.S. Postal Service (USPS) continues to bleed red ink. It reported a net loss of $5.6 billion for fiscal 2016, the 10th straight year its expenses have exceeded revenue. Meanwhile, it has more than $120 billion in unfunded liabilities, mostly for employee health and retirement costs.

> Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze between technological change that has permanently decreased demand for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail, and a regulatory structure that denies management the flexibility to adjust its operations to the new reality.

> And interest groups ranging from postal unions to greeting-card makers exert self-interested pressure on the USPS's ultimate overseer—Congress—insisting that whatever else happens to the Postal Service, aspects of the status quo they depend on get protected. … leaving the Postal Service unable to pay its bills except by deferring vital modernization.

**可回溯语言点**：`bleed red ink`（亏损）、`unfunded liabilities`（无资金准备的负债）、`a historic squeeze`（历史性挤压）、`bread-and-butter product`（拳头/饭碗产品）、`a regulatory structure`（监管架构）、`exert pressure on`（对…施压）、`the status quo`（现状）、`deferring vital modernization`（推迟必要的现代化）、`regulatory`（主题词）。

## 2019 · 英语一

### 阅读 Text 1｜银行高管与长期主义（经济与商业）
> Financial regulators in Britain have imposed a rather unusual rule on the bosses of big banks. Starting next year, any guaranteed bonus of top executives could be delayed 10 years if their banks are under investigation for wrongdoing. The main purpose of this “clawback” rule is to hold bankers accountable for harmful risk-taking and to restore public trust in financial institutions.

> “Short-termism,” or the desire for quick profits, has worsened in publicly traded companies, says the Bank of England's top economist, Andrew Haldane. He quotes a giant of classical economics, Alfred Marshall, in describing this financial impatience as acting like “children who pick the plums out of their pudding to eat them at once” rather than putting them aside to be eaten last.

> Transient investors, who demand high quarterly profits from companies, can hinder a firm's efforts to invest in long-term research or to build up customer loyalty. This has been dubbed “quarterly capitalism.”

> “There seems to be a predominance of short-term thinking at the expense of long-term investing,” said Commissioner Daniel Gallagher of the US Securities and Exchange Commission in a speech this week.

> Much more could be done to encourage “long-termism,” such as changes in the tax code and quicker disclosure of stock acquisitions. … Within companies, the right compensation design can provide incentives for executives to think beyond their own time at the company and on behalf of all stakeholders. Britain's new rule is a reminder to bankers that society has an interest in their performance, not just for the short term but for the long term.

**可回溯语言点**：`impose a rule on`（对…强制推行规定）、`under investigation for wrongdoing`（因不当行为受查）、`clawback`（奖金追回）、`hold bankers accountable for`（让…担责）、`short-termism`（短期主义，主题词）、`a giant of classical economics`（巨匠，非「巨人」）、`transient investors`（短线投资者）、`dub`（把…称为）、`a predominance of`（…占主导）、`at the expense of`（以…为代价）、`the tax code`（税法，code = 法典）、`on behalf of`（代表）、`stakeholders`（利益相关者）、`incentives`（激励，主题词）、`society has an interest in`（interest = 利益）。

### 阅读 Text 2｜大学成绩通胀与「成绩宽恕」（教育与人才）
> Grade inflation—the gradual increase in average GPAs (grade-point averages) over the past few decades—is often considered a product of a consumer era in higher education, in which students are treated like customers to be pleased. But another, related force—a policy often buried deep in course catalogs called “grade forgiveness”—is helping raise GPAs.

> The use of this little-known practice has accelerated in recent years, as colleges continue to do their utmost to keep students in school (and paying tuition) and improve their graduation rates. … But now most colleges, save for many selective campuses, allow all undergraduates, and even graduate students, to get their low grades forgiven.

> College officials tend to emphasize that the goal of grade forgiveness is less about the grade itself and more about encouraging students to retake courses critical to their degree program and graduation without incurring a big penalty. … For public institutions, state funds are sometimes tied partly to their success on metrics such as graduation rates and student retention—so better grades can, by boosting figures like those, mean more money.

> Since students and parents expect a college degree to lead to a job, it is in the best interest of a school to turn out graduates who are as qualified as possible—or at least appear to be. On this, students' and colleges' incentives seem to be aligned.

**可回溯语言点**：`grade inflation`（成绩通胀）、`buried deep in course catalogs`（深藏于课程目录）、`do their utmost`（竭尽全力）、`save for`（除了，非「拯救」）、`incurring a big penalty`（招致重罚）、`metrics`（衡量指标）、`student retention`（学生留存率）、`in the best interest of`（符合…最大利益）、`aligned`（一致的）、`be tied to`（与…挂钩）。

### 阅读 Text 3｜AI 与伦理准则（科技与AI）
> This year marks exactly two centuries since the publication of Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus, by Mary Shelley. Even before the invention of the electric light bulb, the author produced a remarkable work of speculative fiction that would foreshadow many ethical questions to be raised by technologies yet to come.

> Today the rapid growth of artificial intelligence (AI) raises fundamental questions: “What is intelligence, identity, or consciousness? What makes humans humans?” … What is being called artificial general intelligence, machines that would imitate the way humans think, continues to evade scientists.

> Just how people think is still far too complex to be understood, let alone reproduced, says David Eagleman, a Stanford University neuroscientist. … But that doesn't mean crucial ethical issues involving AI aren't at hand. The coming use of autonomous vehicles, for example, poses thorny ethical questions.

> Whenever decisions are based on masses of data, “you quickly get into a lot of ethical questions,” notes Tan Kiat How, chief executive of a Singapore-based agency that is helping the government develop a voluntary code for the ethical use of AI. Along with Singapore, other governments and mega-corporations are beginning to establish their own guidelines.

> On June 7 Google pledged not to “design or deploy AI” that would cause “overall harm,” or to develop AI-directed weapons or use AI for surveillance that would violate international norms. … While the statement is vague, it represents one starting point. ### 阅读 Text 3｜科技与AI（互联网与记忆 / 认知卸载）
> The Internet may be changing merely what we remember, not our capacity to do so, suggests Columbia University psychology professor Betsy Sparrow. ## 2024 · 英语二

### 阅读 Text 1｜科技与AI（数字经济、AI 与社会认同）
> In her new book Cogs and Monsters: What Economics Is, and What It Should Be, Diane Coyle, an economist at Cambridge University, argues that the digital economy requires new ways of thinking about progress. "Whatever we mean by the economy growing, by things getting better, the gains will have to be more evenly shared than in the recent past," she writes. "An economy of tech millionaires or billionaires and gig workers, with middle-income jobs undercut by automation, will not be politically sustainable." Improving living standards and increasing prosperity for more people will require greater use of digital technologies to boost productivity in various sectors ... But people can't be expected to embrace the changes if they're not seeing the benefits—if they're just seeing good jobs being destroyed. In a recent interview, Coyle said she fears that tech's inequality problem could be a roadblock to deploying AI. "We're talking about disruption," she says. "These are transformative technologies that change the ways we spend our time every day, that change business models that succeed." To make such "tremendous changes," she adds, you need social buy-in. Instead, says Coyle, resentment is simmering among many as the benefits are perceived to go to elites in a handful of prosperous cities. In the US, for instance, during much of the 20th century the various regions of the country were—in the language of economists—"converging," and financial disparities decreased. Then, in the 1980s, came the onslaught of digital technologies, and the trend reversed itself. Automation wiped out many manufacturing and retail jobs. New, well-paying tech jobs were clustered in a few cities. ... The dominance of a few cities in the invention and commercialization of AI means that geographical disparities in wealth will continue to soar. Not only will this foster political and social unrest, but it could ... hold back the sorts of AI technologies needed for regional economies to grow. Part of the solution could lie in somehow loosening the stranglehold that Big Tech has on defining the AI agenda. ... ## 2025 · 英语二

### 阅读 Text 1｜经济与商业（小费通胀 tipflation）
> U.S. customers historically tipped people they assumed were earning most of their income via tips, such as restaurant servers earning less than the minimum wage. In the early 2010s, a wide range of businesses started processing purchases with iPads and other digital payment systems. These systems often prompted customers to tip for services that were not previously tipped. Today's tip requests are often not connected to the salary and service norms that used to determine when and how people tip. Customers in the past nearly always paid tips after receiving a service, such as at the conclusion of a restaurant meal, after getting a haircut or once a pizza was delivered. That timing could reward high-quality service and give workers an incentive to provide it. It's becoming more common for tips to be requested beforehand. And new tipping technology may even automatically add tips. The prevalence of digital payment devices has made it easier to ask customers for a tip. ... The amounts have risen from 10% or less in the 1950s to 15% around the year 2000 to 20% or higher today. This increase is sometimes called tipflation—the expectation of ever-higher tip amounts. ... Tipping has always been a vital source of income for workers in historically tipped services, like restaurants, where the tipped minimum wage can be as low as US $2.13 an hour. ... To ensure that all employees were paid fair wages, some restaurants banned tipping and increased prices, but this movement toward no-tipping services has largely fizzled out.

- **2017 Text 1「lever / level」**：启航「legacy 真题复现」、文都法硕等作 `lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches`，中公、启航真题页、可可英语等作 `level ...`（疑为 OCR 沿袭）。因该动词各来源冲突，本卷 `legacy` 条目[ e044 ]只取无争议的前两句连续原文，不引用含 lever/level 的后句。

## 经济与商业

### 主题词（theme）

- **short-termism** /ˌʃɔːt ˈtɜːmɪzəm/ `n.` — 主题义：短期主义（追逐快利而牺牲长远）
  - 真题出处：2019 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2019
  - 原句摘录：
    > “Short-termism,” or the desire for quick profits, has worsened in publicly traded companies, says the Bank of England's top economist, Andrew Haldane.
    > 英格兰银行首席经济学家安德鲁·霍尔丹说，「短期主义」、即对快速获利的渴求，在上市公司中已愈发严重。
  - 要点：2019 英一 Text 1：or the desire for quick profits 是同位释义；写作可与 long-term investment 对举。

- **incentive** /ɪnˈsentɪv/ `n.` — 主题义：激励、动机、刺激（机制）
  - 真题出处：2025 英语二 阅读 Text 1｜freq 3｜years 2019、2021、2025
  - 原句摘录：
    > That timing could reward high-quality service and give workers an incentive to provide it.
    > 这一时机既能奖励优质服务，也能给服务人员提供继续提供优质服务的激励。
  - 要点：give workers an incentive to provide it（2025 英二 Text 1）、provide incentives for executives（2019 英一 Text 1）、had the power and incentive to favor...（2021 英一 Text 4）：三卷复现，incentive to do / incentives for sb 为高频搭配。

- **prevalence** /ˈprevələns/ `n.` — 主题义：普及、盛行、普遍存在
  - 真题出处：2025 英语二 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2025
  - 原句摘录：
    > The prevalence of digital payment devices has made it easier to ask customers for a tip.
    > 数字支付设备的普及，使得向顾客索要小费变得更容易。
  - 要点：The prevalence of digital payment devices（2025 英二 Text 1）：prevalence 是 pervasive（无处不在的）的名词形式，the prevalence of X has made it easier to... 可迁移写作。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **weather** /ˈweðə(r)/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 天气、气象 ｜**僻义**：v. 安全渡过、挺过（困难时期）
  - 真题出处：2007 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2007
  - 原句摘录：
    > the safety net offered by unemployment insurance or disability insurance to help families weather bad times.
    > 由失业保险或伤残保险提供的安全网，帮助家庭渡过艰难时期。
  - 要点：weather bad times / weather the storm 为固定搭配；名词「天气」转动词「经受住风雨」，是典型名动转用僻义。

- **drag** /dræɡ/ `n.` — 常见义：v. 拖、拽 ｜**僻义**：n. 阻碍、负担、累赘（a drag on）
  - 真题出处：2017 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2017
  - 原句摘录：
    > Wasted time is a drag on Americans' economic and private lives, not to mention infuriating.
    > 浪费的时间是美国人经济与私人生活的累赘，更别提还令人恼火。
  - 要点：Wasted time is a drag on Americans' economic and private lives：a drag on 后接「生活/增长/效率」等抽象名词，写作可替换 burden / hindrance。

- **interest** /ˈɪntrəst/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 兴趣、趣味；关注 ｜**僻义**：n. 利益、利害关系；利息（interest rates）
  - 真题出处：2019 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 2｜years 2010、2019
  - 原句摘录：
    > Since students and parents expect a college degree to lead to a job, it is in the best interest of a school to turn out graduates who are as qualified as possible—or at least appear to be.
    > 既然学生和家长指望大学文凭带来工作，培养出尽可能合格的毕业生——或至少看起来合格——就符合学校的最大利益。
  - 要点：in the best interest of（符合…的最大利益）、economic self-interest（经济私利）、interest rates（利率，2010 英二 Text 1）三处义项各异，均非「兴趣」。

- **stake** /steɪk/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 桩、柱；赌注 ｜**僻义**：n. 利害关系、风险（at stake 利害攸关）
  - 真题出处：2022 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2022
  - 原句摘录：
    > Old objects continue to deteriorate. Worse, biodegradable plastics, designed to disintegrate, are increasingly common. And more is at stake here than individual objects.
    > 旧物件持续劣化；更糟的是，为易于降解而设计的可生物降解塑料日益普遍。而这里利害攸关的还不只是单件物品。
  - 要点：more is at stake here than individual objects = 利害所关远不止单件物品；at stake / have a stake in / stakeholder 为一族高频搭配。

- **stem** /stem/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 茎、干；v. 堵住、遏制 ｜**僻义**：v. 起源于、由…造成（stem from）
  - 真题出处：2011 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2011
  - 原句摘录：
    > The way consumers now approach the process of making purchase decisions means that marketing's impact stems from a broad range of factors beyond conventional paid media.
    > 消费者如今作出购买决策的方式意味着，营销的影响力源自传统付费媒体之外的一系列广泛因素。
  - 要点：marketing's impact stems from a broad range of factors：stem from 表因果溯源，写作可替换 derive from / arise from；注意与 stem the flow（遏制）反义。

- **value** /ˈvæljuː/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 价值、价值观；v. 重视、珍视 ｜**僻义**：v. 给…估价、按某价格估值（value assets at ...）
  - 真题出处：2010 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > These rules say they must value some assets at the price a third party would pay, not the price managers and regulators would like them to fetch.
    > 这些准则要求银行按第三方愿意支付的价格为某些资产估值，而非管理层和监管者希望它们能卖出的价格。
  - 要点：value some assets at the price a third party would pay：银行会计语境里 value 是「估值」而非「重视」；value sth at + 价格 是固定搭配。

- **fetch** /fetʃ/ `v.` — 常见义：v. 去取来、拿来；接（人） ｜**僻义**：v. 售得、卖到（某价格）
  - 真题出处：2010 英语二 阅读 Text 1｜freq 2｜years 2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist.
    > 除两件外全部售出，成交价逾七千万英镑，创下单一艺术家专场拍卖的纪录。
  - 要点：fetching more than £70m（2010 英二 Text 1 拍卖）、the price ... would like them to fetch（2010 英一 Text 4 资产估值）：同年两卷同考「售得」，是跨卷复现的典型。

- **hedge** /hedʒ/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 树篱、篱笆；v. 回避正面回答 ｜**僻义**：v. 对冲（金融风险）、套期保值
  - 真题出处：2010 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 1｜years 2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > The Bilski case involves a claimed patent on a method for hedging risk in the energy market.
    > 比尔斯基案涉及一项声称的专利，即一种在能源市场对冲风险的方法。
  - 要点：a method for hedging risk in the energy market（2010 英一 Text 2 Bilski 案）：金融法律语境的专门义，hedge against inflation 可迁移。

  - 真题出处：2018 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2018

### 主题词（theme）

- **legitimacy** /lɪˈdʒɪtɪməsi/ `n.` — 主题义：合法性、正当性（制度被认可的程度）
  - 真题出处：2012 英语一 完形｜freq 2｜years 2012、2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > The court cannot maintain its legitimacy as guardian of the rule of law when justices behave like politicians.
    > 当大法官们表现得像政客时，最高法院便无法维系其作为法治守护者的合法性。
  - 要点：maintain its legitimacy as guardian of the rule of law（2012 英一 完形）、the legal ecosystem has lost legitimacy among its users（2020 英一 Text 2）：两卷均指制度的正当性，非 legal（合法）本身。

- **regulatory** /ˌreɡjuˈleɪtəri/ `adj.` — 主题义：监管的、管制的（regulatory structure）
  - 真题出处：2018 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2018
  - 原句摘录：
    > Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze between technological change that has permanently decreased demand for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail, and a regulatory structure that denies management the flexibility to adjust its operations to the new reality.
    > 根本上，美国邮政陷入历史性挤压：一边是技术变革永久压低了对其拳头产品——一类邮件——的需求，一边是监管架构剥夺了管理层按新现实调整经营的灵活性。
  - 要点：a regulatory structure that denies management the flexibility（2018 英一 Text 4 USPS）：写作可用 regulatory framework / regulatory burden 讨论政府与市场边界。

- **curb** /kɜːb/ `v./n.` — 主题义：抑制、遏制、约束
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 2｜years 2013、2018
  - 原句摘录：
    > Though several fast-fashion companies have made efforts to curb their impact on labor and the environment—including H&M, with its green Conscious Collection line—Cline believes lasting change can only be effected by the customer.
    > 尽管数家快时尚公司已努力遏制其对劳工与环境的影响——包括 H&M 推出绿色'自觉系列'——Cline 认为，持久的改变唯有由消费者来促成。
  - 要点：made efforts to curb their impact（2013 英一 Text 1）、to curb the tech giants（2018 英一 Text 3）：curb + 抽象对象（影响/巨头/排放）是环境、治理议题高频动词。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **subject** /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt/ `v./adj.` — 常见义：n. 主题、学科、主语 ｜**僻义**：v. 使遭受、使经受；adj. 受制于、须经（subject to）
  - 真题出处：2008 英语一 完形｜freq 2｜years 2008、2012
  - 原句摘录：
    > His argument is that the unusual history of these people has subjected them to unique evolutionary pressures that have resulted in this paradoxical state of affairs.
    > 他的论点是，这群人不同寻常的历史使他们承受了独特的进化压力，从而造就了这一看似矛盾的状况。
  - 要点：subject sb to sth（使…经受）、be subject to（受…约束/须经）；2008 完形考 subjected them to pressures，2012 完形考 make itself subject to the code。

- **buy** /baɪ/ `v.` — 常见义：v. 购买、买 ｜**僻义**：v. 相信、接受（说法/借口）；（被动 be bought）被收买、被买通
  - 真题出处：2005 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 2｜years 2005、2017
  - 原句摘录：
    > Lots of Americans bought that nonsense, and over three decades, some 10 million smokers went to early graves.
    > 许多美国人信了那套胡言，结果三十多年间约有一千万烟民早早进了坟墓。
  - 要点：2005 英一 Text 2 bought that nonsense = 相信了那套胡说；2017 英一 Text 4 connections can be bought = 关系能被收买。及物动词后接抽象对象是判断僻义的信号。

- **code** /kəʊd/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 密码、编码、邮政编码 ｜**僻义**：n. 行为准则、法典、规范（ethics code / voluntary code）
  - 真题出处：2012 英语一 完形｜freq 2｜years 2012、2019
  - 原句摘录：
    > Part of the problem is that the justices are not bound by an ethics code.
    > 问题的部分症结在于，大法官们并不受伦理准则的约束。
  - 要点：2012 完形 ethics code（伦理准则）、2019 Text 3 voluntary code for the ethical use of AI（AI 伦理自愿规范）；法律/治理语境一律不作「密码」解。

- **fashion** /ˈfæʃn/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 时尚、时装、方式 ｜**僻义**：v.（精心）制定、塑造、做成（政策/制度）
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > Arizona had attempted to fashion state policies that ran parallel to the existing federal ones.
    > 亚利桑那州曾试图制定与现行联邦政策并行的本州政策。
  - 要点：Arizona had attempted to fashion state policies：fashion 与 policies 搭配只能是「制定/塑造」；注意与 in a ... fashion（以…方式）区分。

- **ground** /ɡraʊnd/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 地面、土地；场地 ｜**僻义**：n.（复数 grounds）理由、根据、依据（grounds for / on the grounds that）
  - 真题出处：2014 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 2｜years 2014、2021
  - 原句摘录：
    > But there are few places where clients have more grounds for complaint than America.
    > 但几乎没有哪个地方，委托人会比在美国更有理由抱怨。
  - 要点：grounds for complaint（抱怨的理由）、on the grounds that（以…为理由/因为）为高频法政搭配；单数 ground 表「地面」，复数才表「依据」。

- **letter** /ˈletə(r)/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 信、函；字母 ｜**僻义**：n. 字面意义（to the letter 严格照字面/不折不扣）
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > The White House argued that Arizona's laws conflicted with its enforcement priorities, even if state laws complied with federal statutes to the letter.
    > 白宫辩称，即便州法不折不扣地符合联邦法规，亚利桑那州的法律仍与其执法优先次序相冲突。
  - 要点：complied with federal statutes to the letter = 逐字逐条严格遵从联邦法规；the letter of the law（条文字面）与 the spirit（立法精神）对举。

- **term** /tɜːm/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 学期；术语；期限 ｜**僻义**：n.（复数）条款、条件、（协议/审判赖以进行的）条件
  - 真题出处：2015 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2015
  - 原句摘录：
    > In many respects, the dearth of moral purpose frames not only the fact of such widespread phone hacking but the terms on which the trial took place.
    > 在许多方面，道德宗旨的缺失不仅框定了如此普遍的电话窃听这一事实，也框定了审判得以展开的条件。
  - 要点：the terms on which the trial took place = 审判得以展开的条件/条款；terms of an agreement、come to terms 义项各异，法政语境优先取「条款」。

- **warrant** /ˈwɒrənt/ `n.` — 常见义：v. 使有必要、值得；n. 保证、正当理由 ｜**僻义**：n.（法院/法官签发的）令状、搜查令
  - 真题出处：2015 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 1｜years 2015
  - 原句摘录：
    > The Supreme Court will now consider whether police can search the contents of a mobile phone without a warrant if the phone is on or around a person during an arrest.
    > 最高法院如今将审议：逮捕时若手机在嫌疑人身上或近旁，警方能否在无搜查令的情况下查看手机内容。
  - 要点：search the contents of a mobile phone without a warrant：2015 英一 Text 2 考数字时代搜查令，warrant 为法律术语，不是「保证/使有必要」。

- **court** /kɔːt/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 庭院、球场；v. 求爱、试图博得 ｜**僻义**：n. 法院、法庭（the Supreme Court / federal court）
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 2｜years 2010、2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > On a five to three vote, the Supreme Court knocked out much of Arizona's immigration law Monday—a modest policy victory for the Obama Administration.
    > 最高法院周一以五比三的票数推翻了亚利桑那州移民法的大部分条款——这是奥巴马政府一次不算大的政策胜利。
  - 要点：the Supreme Court knocked out much of Arizona's immigration law；2010 英一 Text 2 另见 Supreme Court / top patent court。法政阅读中 court 一律指法院，勿按「庭院/求爱」理解。

- **fine** /faɪn/ `n.` — 常见义：adj. 好的、精美的、细微的；adv. 很好地 ｜**僻义**：n. 罚款、罚金（levy fines / pay a fine）
  - 真题出处：2020 英语二 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > A judge has now overruled the city's decision to stop levying fines, ordering them reinstated.
    > 一名法官现已推翻该市停止征收罚金的决定，并下令恢复征收。
  - 要点：stop levying fines, ordering them reinstated（2020 英二 Text 3 清洁空气区）：fine 与 levy（征收）搭配只能是「罚金」，与「好的/细微的」毫无关系。

- **provisions** /prəˈvɪʒnz/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 供应、准备；（复数）食品储备 ｜**僻义**：n.（法律、协议的）条款、规定
  - 真题出处：2020 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 2｜years 2013、2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > Instead, the digital services tax is part of a much larger trend, with countries over the past few years proposing or putting in place an alphabet soup of new international tax provisions.
    > 相反，数字服务税是一个更大趋势的一部分：过去几年各国纷纷提出或实施一大堆以字母缩写命名的国际税收新条款。
  - 要点：contested provisions of Arizona's plan（2013 英一 Text 4）、new international tax provisions（2020 英一 Text 4）两处均指法条条款；与「食品储备」义落差大。

- **frame** /freɪm/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 框架、相框、骨架 ｜**僻义**：v. 界定、框定；构陷、诬陷
  - 真题出处：2015 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2015
  - 原句摘录：
    > In many respects, the dearth of moral purpose frames not only the fact of such widespread phone hacking but the terms on which the trial took place.
    > 在许多方面，道德宗旨的缺失不仅框定了如此普遍的电话窃听这一事实，也框定了审判得以展开的条件。
  - 要点：frames not only the fact ... but the terms on which the trial took place：frame 作动词表「框定/界定」；新闻语境还可表「诬陷」（be framed）。

- **upset** /ʌpˈset/ `v.` — 常见义：adj. 心烦的；v. 使难过、使不适 ｜**僻义**：v. 打乱、颠覆、推翻（平衡、假定、秩序）
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > But on the more important matter of the Constitution, the decision was an 8-0 defeat for the Administration's effort to upset the balance of power between the federal government and the states.
    > 但在更为重要的宪法问题上，这一判决以八比零挫败了政府试图打破联邦与州之间权力平衡的努力。
  - 要点：upset the balance of power between the federal government and the states（2013 英一 Text 4）：宾语是抽象「平衡」，义为「颠覆」，非「使难过」。


## 教育与人才

### 主题词（theme）

- **standardized testing** /ˈstændədaɪzd/ `n. phrase` — 主题义：标准化考试（统一命题、统一评分）
  - 真题出处：2009 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2009
  - 原句摘录：
    > The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.
    > 当下对标准化考试的强调凸显了分析与程序，意味着我们中很少有人会本能地运用创新与协作的思维模式。
  - 要点：The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure（2009 英一 Text 1）：与 innovative and collaborative modes of thought 对举，教育改革议题常考。

- **productivity** /ˌprɒdʌkˈtɪvəti/ `n.` — 主题义：劳动生产率、生产率
  - 真题出处：2022 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 2｜years 2009、2022
  - 原句摘录：
    > As countless boards and business owners will attest, constraining firms from firing poorly performing, high-earning managers is a handbrake on boosting productivity and overall performance.
    > 正如无数董事会和企业主会证实的那样，限制企业解雇表现不佳的高薪经理，无异于给提升生产率和整体绩效踩了手刹。
  - 要点：automotive-assembly productivity（2009 英一 Text 3）、a handbrake on boosting productivity（本句，2022 英一 Text 4）：两卷复现，常见搭配 boost / constrain productivity。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **practice** /ˈpræktɪs/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 练习、实践；v. 练习 ｜**僻义**：n. 惯例、惯常做法、（机构通行的）操作方式
  - 真题出处：2020 英语二 阅读 Text 2｜freq 3｜years 2018、2019、2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > And the stock market reacts positively when companies tie CEO pay to, say, stock prices, a sign that those practices build up corporate value not just for the CEO.
    > 当企业把 CEO 薪酬与股价等挂钩时，股市反应积极——这表明此类做法提升的是整个公司的价值，而不只是 CEO 的。
  - 要点：the use of this little-known practice（2019 英一 Text 2 成绩宽恕）、This practice does not address...（2018 英一 Text 3）、those practices build up corporate value（本句）：三卷均指制度性做法，非「练习」。

- **save** /seɪv/ `prep.` — 常见义：v. 拯救、节省、储蓄 ｜**僻义**：prep. 除…之外，等于 except for
  - 真题出处：2019 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 1｜years 2019
  - 原句摘录：
    > But now most colleges, save for many selective campuses, allow all undergraduates, and even graduate students, to get their low grades forgiven.
    > 但如今除许多选拔性强的院校外，多数高校允许所有本科生、甚至研究生把低分「宽恕」掉。
  - 要点：save for many selective campuses = 除少数择优录取的院校之外；save (for) 作介词是正式书面用法，考生极易误读为动词「拯救」。

- **sound** /saʊnd/ `adj.` — 常见义：n. 声音；v. 听起来（系动词） ｜**僻义**：adj. 扎实可靠的、健全的、无懈可击的
  - 真题出处：2023 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2023
  - 原句摘录：
    > “What millions of Texas kids learn in their public schools is determined too often by the political ideology of partisan board members, rather than facts and sound scholarship.”
    > 「数百万得州孩子在公立学校学到什么，往往由党派倾向的委员的政治意识形态决定，而非事实与严谨的学术。」
  - 要点：facts and sound scholarship 中 sound 修饰 scholarship，意为「扎实严谨的学术」；a sound policy / sound judgment 同类，写作可替换 reliable / rigorous。


## 环境与能源

### 主题词（theme）

- **biomass** /ˈbaɪəʊmæs/ `n.` — 主题义：（某区域/物种的）生物量
  - 真题出处：2006 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2006
  - 原句摘录：
    > Their methods do not attempt to estimate the actual biomass (the amount of living biological matter) of fish species in particular parts of the ocean, but rather changes in that biomass over time.
    > 他们的方法并不试图估算特定海域鱼类物种的实际生物量，而是估算该生物量随时间发生的变化。
  - 要点：estimate the actual biomass of fish species（2006 英一 Text 3）：渔业可持续产量语境的专业术语，与 maximum sustainable yield 同篇。

- **mitigation** /ˌmɪtɪˈɡeɪʃn/ `n.` — 主题义：减缓、缓解（措施），（气候）减缓
  - 真题出处：2023 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2023
  - 原句摘录：
    > These would emphasise the primacy of human activity in recent climate change and encourage discussion of mitigation measures.
    > 这些标准将强调人类活动在近期气候变化中的首要作用，并鼓励讨论减缓措施。
  - 要点：encourage discussion of mitigation measures（2023 英一 Text 1 气候教材标准争议）：与 adaptation（适应）并列为气候政策两大术语。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **game** /ɡeɪm/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 游戏、比赛、一局 ｜**僻义**：n. 猎物、野味（集合名词）；引申（one's game）本领、行当
  - 真题出处：2010 英语一 翻译｜freq 2｜years 2010、2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > these creatures preserve the health of game by killing the physically weak
    > 这些生物通过猎杀体弱者来维持猎物种群的健康
  - 要点：2010 英一翻译 preserve the health of game 考「猎物」；2020 英二 Text 2 CEOs ... upped their game 考「提升本领/表现」。相隔十年两卷复现，义项落差极大。

- **yield** /jiːld/ `n.` — 常见义：v. 屈服、让步；产生（结果） ｜**僻义**：n. 产出、产量（maximum sustainable yield）
  - 真题出处：2006 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2006
  - 原句摘录：
    > That matters because theory suggests that the maximum sustainable yield that can be cropped from a fishery comes when the biomass of a target species is about 50% of its original levels.
    > 这一点很重要，因为理论表明，一个渔场可捕取的最大可持续产量，出现在目标物种生物量约为其原始水平 50% 之时。
  - 要点：the maximum sustainable yield that can be cropped from a fishery：2006 英一 Text 3 渔业语境，yield 作名词表「产量」，与「屈服」落差极大。


## 社会与人口

### 主题词（theme）

- **assimilation** /əˌsɪməˈleɪʃn/ `n.` — 主题义：（移民的）同化、融入主流社会
  - 真题出处：2006 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2006
  - 原句摘录：
    > Now, consider three indices of assimilation -- language, home ownership and intermarriage.
    > 现在来看同化的三项指标——语言、住房自有率和异族通婚。
  - 要点：three indices of assimilation -- language, home ownership and intermarriage（2006 英一 Text 1）：以三项指标量化同化程度；与 homogenize（趋同）同源。

- **unprecedented** /ʌnˈpresɪdentɪd/ `adj.` — 主题义：空前的、前所未有的
  - 真题出处：2006 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2006
  - 原句摘录：
    > Writing for the National Immigration Forum, Gregory Rodriguez reports that today's immigration is neither at unprecedented levels nor resistant to assimilation.
    > 格雷戈里·罗德里格斯在为国家移民论坛撰文时指出，当今的移民既未达到空前规模，也并不抗拒同化。
  - 要点：neither at unprecedented levels nor resistant to assimilation（2006 英一 Text 1）：unprecedented + 规模/速度/挑战 是高阶强调，语气强于 new。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **address** /əˈdres/ `v.` — 常见义：n. 地址；v. 向……讲话、发表演说 ｜**僻义**：v. 处理、应对、设法解决（抽象问题）
  - 真题出处：2006 英语一 完形｜freq 3｜years 2006、2016、2018
  - 原句摘录：
    > Boston Globe reporter Chris Reidy notes that the situation will improve only when there are comprehensive programs that address the many needs of the homeless.
    > 《波士顿环球报》记者克里斯·里迪指出，只有出现能应对无家可归者诸多需求的综合性项目，状况才会改善。
  - 要点：address + 抽象名词（needs / misuse / worry）是考研高频动词化僻义，写作中比 deal with 更正式；三卷复现（2006 完形、2016 Text 1、2018 Text 3）。

- **cure** /kjʊə(r)/ `n.` — 常见义：v. 治愈（疾病）；n. 药物、疗法 ｜**僻义**：n.（社会问题的）对策、解决办法（the social cure）
  - 真题出处：2012 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 2｜years 2012、2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > The most glaring flaw of the social cure as it's presented here is that it doesn't work very well for very long.
    > 此处所呈现的'社会疗法'最显眼的缺陷，是它并不能长久奏效。
  - 要点：2012 Text 1 the social cure 指「靠同辈压力改造社会的整套方案」，2013 Text 3 cure all the ills of humanity 用其动词「消除」义；由治病引申为治社会痼疾。

- **party** /ˈpɑːti/ `n./v.` — 常见义：n. 聚会；政党 ｜**僻义**：n.（法律/交易）一方、当事人；v. 参加社交聚会、与…交际
  - 真题出处：2015 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 2｜years 2010、2015
  - 原句摘录：
    > Even so, these are wealthy families who party with the international 1%, and media intrusiveness makes it increasingly difficult to maintain the right image.
    > 即便如此，这些仍是与国际顶层 1% 阶层往来酬酢的富裕家族，而媒体的无孔不入使其越来越难维持得体形象。
  - 要点：a third party would pay（2010 英一 Text 4）中 party = 交易第三方；2015 英一 Text 1 party with the international 1% 则是动词「与…交际」。两卷义项不同，均为僻义。

- **preserve** /prɪˈzɜːv/ `n.` — 常见义：v. 保护、保存、腌制；n. 果酱 ｜**僻义**：n.（the preserve of）某群体的专属领域、独享之物
  - 真题出处：2005 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 2｜years 2005、2021
  - 原句摘录：
    > Feelings of righteous indignation, it seems, are not the preserve of people alone.
    > 看来，义愤之情并非人类所独有。
  - 要点：are not the preserve of people alone（2005 英一 Text 1）、the preserve of the super-rich（2021 英一 Text 3）两处均指「专属/专利」；注意 the + preserve + of 结构。


## 心理与行为

### 主题词（theme）

- **peer pressure** /ˈpɪə ˌpreʃə(r)/ `n. phrase` — 主题义：同辈压力、朋辈压力
  - 真题出处：2012 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2012
  - 原句摘录：
    > This is a subtle form of peer pressure: we unconsciously imitate the behavior we see every day.
    > 这是同辈压力的一种微妙形式：我们会无意识地模仿每天所见的行为。
  - 要点：a subtle form of peer pressure（2012 英一 Text 1）：与 the social cure 同篇，指群体无意识模仿；注意与 peer review（同行评议）区分。

- **well-being** /ˌwel ˈbiːɪŋ/ `n.` — 主题义：福祉、幸福感、安康
  - 真题出处：2017 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2017
  - 原句摘录：
    > A recent annual study of countries and their ability to convert growth into well-being sheds some light on that question.
    > 一项关于各国把增长转化为福祉之能力的最新年度研究，为这一问题提供了一些线索。
  - 要点：convert growth into well-being（2017 英一 Text 3）：把增长转化为国民福祉，是「增长之外」议题的核心术语。

- **vulnerable** /ˈvʌlnərəbl/ `adj.` — 主题义：脆弱的、易受伤害/易被推翻的
  - 真题出处：2020 英语二 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > Among other weaknesses, the measures cities must employ when left to tackle dirty air on their own are politically contentious, and therefore vulnerable.
    > 在诸多弱点中，城市独自应对空气污染时不得不采取的措施在政治上易受争议，因而十分脆弱、易被推翻。
  - 要点：politically contentious, and therefore vulnerable（2020 英二 Text 3）：指地方治污措施因政治争议而「易被废除」；be vulnerable to + 风险 为高频搭配。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **constant** /ˈkɒnstənt/ `n.` — 常见义：adj. 持续不断的、恒定的 ｜**僻义**：n. 恒量、恒定不变的事物
  - 真题出处：2013 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2013
  - 原句摘录：
    > Vanity is a constant; people will only start shopping more sustainably when they can't afford not to.
    > 虚荣心是恒常不变的；只有当人们负担不起'不这样做'时，才会开始更可持续地消费。
  - 要点：Vanity is a constant 中 constant 作名词表语，意为「恒常不变的东西」；形容词转名词是考研常见僻义路径。


## 传媒与文化

### 主题词（theme）

- **earned media** /ˈɜːnd ˈmiːdiə/ `n. phrase` — 主题义：赢得型媒体（消费者自发推荐形成的口碑传播）
  - 真题出处：2011 英语一 阅读 Text 3｜freq 1｜years 2011
  - 原句摘录：
    > Consumers passionate about a product may create "earned" media by willingly promoting it to friends, and a company may leverage "owned" media by sending e-mail alerts about products and sales to customers registered with its Web site.
    > 对某产品满怀热情的消费者可能通过自愿向朋友推荐而创造'赢得型'媒体，企业则可借向网站注册用户发送产品与促销邮件提醒来运营'自有型'媒体。
  - 要点：create "earned" media by willingly promoting it to friends（2011 英一 Text 3）：与 owned / paid media 对举，营销传播术语。

- **media literacy** /ˈmiːdiə ˈlɪtərəsi/ `n. phrase` — 主题义：媒介素养（辨别、评估媒体信息的能力）
  - 真题出处：2018 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 1｜years 2018
  - 原句摘录：
    > Yet as distrust has risen toward all media, people may be starting to beef up their media literacy skills. Such a trend is badly needed.
    > 然而随着对所有媒体的不信任上升，人们或许正开始增强自身的媒介素养技能；这种趋势亟需。
  - 要点：beef up their media literacy skills（2018 英一 Text 2）：在「对媒体不信任上升」语境中提出，与 misinformation 议题相关。

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **spell** /spel/ `v.` — 常见义：v. 拼写；n. 咒语、一段时间 ｜**僻义**：v. 招致、意味着（坏结果），spell the death of 招致…的消亡
  - 真题出处：2005 英语一 阅读 Text 4｜freq 1｜years 2005
  - 原句摘录：
    > But the cult of the authentic and the personal, "doing our own thing," has spelt the death of formal speech, writing, poetry and music.
    > 然而，对「真实」与「个性」的推崇，即「各行其是」，已经宣告了正式演讲、写作、诗歌和音乐的消亡。
  - 要点：spelt the death of formal speech 中 spell = 带来…的结局，不是「拼写」也不是「一段时期」；spell disaster / spell the end of 可迁移写作。

- **press** /pres/ `v./n.` — 常见义：v. 按、压、挤；n. 按压 ｜**僻义**：v. 敦促、极力要求（press for）；n. 新闻界、报界（the press）
  - 真题出处：2005 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 2｜years 2005、2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > Instead of a plan of action, they continue to press for more research -- a classic case of "paralysis by analysis."
    > 他们拿不出行动计划，反而不断要求更多研究——这是「分析致瘫」的典型案例。
  - 要点：press for more research = 一再要求更多研究；be published in the daily press（2010 英一 Text 1）= 刊于日报/新闻界。动词与名词两处僻义分属不同卷。

- **calling** /ˈkɔːlɪŋ/ `n.` — 常见义：call 的现在分词/动名词：呼叫、打电话 ｜**僻义**：n. 天职、使命感、神召
  - 真题出处：2010 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > These men believed in journalism as a calling, and were proud to be published in the daily press.
    > 这些人把新闻业视为一种天职，并以能在日报上发表文章为荣。
  - 要点：believed in journalism as a calling = 把新闻业当作天职；与 vocation 近义，写作可表达职业认同。

- **ornament** /ˈɔːnəmənt/ `n.` — 常见义：n. 装饰品、点缀物（具体物件） ｜**僻义**：n.（抽象）增色之物、装饰性点缀、门面
  - 真题出处：2010 英语一 阅读 Text 1｜freq 1｜years 2010
  - 原句摘录：
    > at a time when newsprint was dirt-cheap and stylish arts criticism was considered an ornament to the publications in which it appeared.
    > 那时新闻纸便宜得要命，而格调高雅的艺术评论被视为其所刊载刊物的点缀。
  - 要点：stylish arts criticism was considered an ornament to the publications：不是具体摆件，而是「为刊物增色的点缀」；由具体装饰引申为抽象增色。

## 科学与技术

### 主题词（theme）

- **peer review** /ˌpɪə rɪˈvjuː/ `n. phrase` — 主题义：同行评议/同行评审（成果发表前的专家评审）

### 熟词僻义（polyseme）

- **secure** /sɪˈkjʊə(r)/ `v.` — 常见义：adj. 安全的、稳妥的；v. 保卫 ｜**僻义**：v.（经努力）获得、确保取得（资源/资金）
  - 真题出处：2020 英语一 阅读 Text 2｜freq 1｜years 2020
  - 原句摘录：
    > With the content of papers secured for free, the publisher needs only find a market for its journal.
    > 论文内容既然免费到手，出版商只需为期刊找到市场即可。
  - 要点：With the content of papers secured for free：secure 后接具体资源表「弄到手」，写作可替换 obtain / gain；勿读作「使安全」。
